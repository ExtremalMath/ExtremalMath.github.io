Tracking Vehicle

Project report: labour_edu.pdf ↗

This report is about making a tracking car in the labour class. First I learned to solder on a breadboard, then I soldered the car board by hand. During the build I used tools to fix the parts. A plastic part melted near the hot iron, but I finished the car carefully. The car can follow the black line on the floor with two sensors and two motors. This project taught me to think before I act.
