I am Yuanbo Li (Bruce Lee), from Jiangsu, China, an undergraduate at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Jiangsu University. My interests sit, somewhat tentatively, at the intersection of extremal graph theory and the functional-analytic methods used in partial differential equations — though at this stage I am still exploring rather than committing to a single direction.

I came to mathematics in 2023, after reading Titu Andreescu's Mathematical Reflections (2010–2011) and working through a graduate algebra course I followed online. What began as curiosity about individual problems gradually became a sustained interest in the discipline itself.

In the longer term, I hope to pursue research in mathematics and to contribute, in whatever small measure, to the field and to the broader mathematical community.

"厚积薄发，以点破面。" — I believe in accumulating deep knowledge steadily, and using focused breakthroughs to illuminate broader mathematical landscapes.

🔬 Research Interests

Extremal Graph Theory

My primary research focus is on Extremal Graph Theory, with a specific emphasis on H-free graph problems. I am particularly interested in the structural properties of these graphs and the associated optimization problems, including Turán-type extremal problems and related enumeration questions.

● Completed Notes

Notes: Density Conditions for Triangles in Multipartite Graphs

Completed a rigorous reading and detailed note set on Bondy, Shen, Thomasse, and Thomassen's work. Explored edge density thresholds using convex hull theory (Carathéodory's theorem) and minimal counterexample analysis. Key results highlighted include the golden ratio bound (τ ≈ 0.618) for tripartite graphs and the 1/2 threshold for infinite-partite graphs. See notes in the Materials->Course essays

● In Progress

Studying: Functional Analysis for PDEs

Focusing on the intersection of functional analysis and partial differential equations. This area uses Banach and Hilbert space theory, weak topologies and duality, operator and spectral theory, convex analysis, and monotone operator methods to study Sobolev spaces, variational formulations, and the existence, uniqueness, and regularity of weak solutions to elliptic and evolution equations.

Note: My research direction is dynamic and may evolve to explore other areas of discrete mathematics or combinatorics.
