Last Resort（空晓界限）

Punishing: Gray Raven · Chapter reflections.

Plot summary

Karenina is asked by the group in control to restart the Zero-Point Energy machine. But by chance she wakes up an old virus that had been shut away for a long time, and it gets into the engine that keeps the floating city flying. The accident brings back a sad story from the past.

Long ago, Karenina's grandfather and his team worked with the same group on the moon. They used the virus to build a new machine. But when the work was almost done, the virus got out and the whole team died. The last scientist shut himself and the sick ones inside the heart of the machine. So when Karenina restarts the machine, she lets all of this out again without meaning to. The other story is about Luna, a prisoner who had lost all hope. With Roland's help, she slowly finds hope again and learns to face human feelings once more.

While the council is still arguing about whether to use the engine for a long trip into space or to turn it into a weapon, Karenina sends a message from the site: the engine must be destroyed, or both the moon base and the Earth will be in great danger. Both stories ask the same question: are you willing to fight for what you believe in?

What stays with me

  • 和神秘而又高端的印象不同，科学的开发过程在80%的时间里都是无聊且无用的，值得庆祝的成功不过是来自于成千上万次的反复测试。在卡列尼娜小的时候，她的爷爷卡农便经常做着各种荒唐的实验，偶尔……不，绝大多数的情况下都会以失败告终。——但也有时候，非常非常偶尔的时候，在一次惊人的爆炸之后， 能看到浑身熏成漆黑只露出白白牙齿呵呵笑着的卡农，捧着一抹煤灰走出门来。年幼的卡列小心翼翼地托着爷爷的手，轻轻抹去最上面的一层煤灰，便能看到里面蕴藏着无比璀璨的钻石。
  • 在一种理论里，宇宙最开始不过是一个死寂的奇点，是一场巨大的爆炸，才让一切的美好的东西诞生，所以爆炸才是最伟大的力量哈哈哈哈！
  • 或许今天我们还不够强大，最顶尖的技术者们也仍旧像是一个仰望月亮的小孩子一样蒙昧可笑。但我们每天积累的知识绝不会化为无用，终于有一天我们会将科学那诞生于破坏的力量变成新的希望。
  • 她收起了笑容，走向了虽然来自不同的组织，有着不同的信念，却站在彼此之间的技术者们。连眼神都不曾交汇，双方便背对着彼此，走向了属于各自的战场。
