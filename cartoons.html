My hobbies outside mathematics — animation, theme songs, and a little bit of nostalgia.

元气星魂之神龙拯救队 (Star Guardian)

Xiangfeng is a 14-year-old boy who loves adventure. One day, he finds a Star Soul Stone. This wakes up the hero named Lie Kongyan. From that moment on, he takes the duty to protect the Earth. He searches for other Star Soul warriors to fight against the aliens. Many young people join together to form the Dragon Rescue Team. They work well and solve problems together. With their efforts, they keep the peace.

機動戦士ガンダムSEED (Mobile Suit Gundam SEED)

Kira Yamato is a university student who loves peace. His good friend Athrun goes home. One day, while Kira and his friends are at school, ZAFT soldiers come to the school to take the Robot. Kira gets inside the robot and starts to fight. He must face his good friend Athrun in battle. Kira must decide between protecting his friends or following orders. He uses the power of the Seed, the ability to change the future. This is a story about war, friendship, and becoming a better person.
