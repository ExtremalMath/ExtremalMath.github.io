China Undergraduate Mathematical Modeling Competition — Problem A

Sep 2026 · 高教社杯全国大学生数学建模竞赛 A题：药材烘干过程的热质传递建模及应用
Modeling Lead (建模手)

In this competition (Problem A: heat and mass transfer in medicinal-material drying), I served as modeling lead. With AI assistance, I derived and verified the one-dimensional radial heat-conduction and moisture-diffusion equations, coupled them into a heat–mass transfer system, and validated the numerical scheme—Crank–Nicolson finite differences, the Thomas algorithm for tridiagonal systems, and Picard iteration. The programming member handled the coding and part of the Problem 3 modeling; the writing member managed typesetting, the method list, and the results cleared for public release. Held in September 2026, the contest has concluded and the paper received no award; it is shared here as a personal learning record.

Published with the team’s consent; the linked PDF is an anonymized version (school name, member names, and advisor removed).
