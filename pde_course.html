About the Course

Partial Differential Equations (PDE) is a core mathematics course I took in the autumn term of 2026. It covers the classical theory of second-order linear equations — the heat equation, the wave equation, and the Laplace equation — together with the methods used to solve them: separation of variables, Fourier series, Green's functions, and the energy method.

The course built directly on my earlier study of real analysis and functional analysis, and gave me the analytic language I now use when reading extremal graph theory papers that rely on variational or spectral arguments.
